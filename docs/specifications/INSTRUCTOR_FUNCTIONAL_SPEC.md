# INSTRUCTOR / SCIENCE COMMUNICATOR

## Instructor / Science Communicator Portal — Complete Functional Structure

The **Instructor / Science Communicator Portal** is the field-execution layer of the Science on Wheels platform. It is used by instructors/science communicators to carry out the activities assigned to them by the Regional Administrator.

The Instructor does not manage regional planning or organization-level configuration. Their primary responsibility is:

> **Prepare → Travel → Conduct → Record → Reconcile → Report**

The portal should therefore be focused on the instructor's **assigned visits, laboratory, routes, school activities, science sessions, equipment, inventory and field records**.



---



# 1. DASHBOARD

The **Dashboard** is the Instructor's operational home screen.

It should provide a quick view of the instructor's current and upcoming field responsibilities.

### Today's Overview

The dashboard should show:

- Today's assigned visits
- Current school
- Assigned mobile laboratory
- Today's route
- Upcoming session
- Number of sessions to conduct
- Pending tasks
- Inventory/equipment alerts
- Open operational issues

For example:

Today's Visit

School: ABC School

Time: 10:00 AM

Lab: ML-002

Session: Class 9 – Physics

Status: Upcoming

### Current Activity

The instructor should be able to immediately see whether they are:

Not Started

     ↓

Travelling

     ↓

At School

     ↓

Session In Progress

     ↓

Session Completed

### Alerts

Relevant field alerts may include:

- Missing equipment
- Low consumable stock
- Damaged equipment
- Vehicle/lab issue
- Schedule change
- New assignment
- Pending session completion
- Synchronization issue

The dashboard should prioritize information that requires immediate action rather than displaying organization-wide statistics.



---



# 2. MY SCHEDULE

The **My Schedule** section contains only visits and activities assigned to the Instructor.



---



## 2.1 Today's Visits

The Instructor should be able to view all visits scheduled for the current day.

Each visit can show:

- School
- Visit time
- Laboratory
- Route
- Session
- Class
- Subject
- Assigned experiment
- Visit status

Example:

10:00 AM

ABC School

ML-001

Class 9 – Physics

Experiment: Reflection of Light

Status: Scheduled



---



## 2.2 Upcoming Visits

The Instructor should be able to view future assigned visits.

Information includes:

- Date
- Time
- School
- Laboratory
- Session
- Experiment
- Route
- Status

This allows the instructor to prepare equipment and materials before the visit.



---



## 2.3 Assigned Schools

This provides a list of schools associated with the Instructor's assigned visits.

The Instructor should be able to view:

- School name
- Location
- Upcoming visit
- Previous visit
- Session history
- Relevant school information

The Instructor should not be able to modify the organization's school master data.



---



## 2.4 Visit Details

Selecting a visit should open its complete operational information.

It should show:

Visit

 ├── School

 ├── Date / Time

 ├── Laboratory

 ├── Route

 ├── Class

 ├── Subject

 ├── Experiment

 ├── Assigned Staff

 └── Status

The Instructor uses this information to execute the assigned visit.



---



# 3. MY MOBILE LABORATORY

This section provides the Instructor with information about the laboratory assigned to them.

The Instructor should primarily **use and verify** the laboratory resources rather than configure the organization's laboratory master data.



---



## 3.1 Lab Details

The Instructor should be able to view:

- Laboratory ID
- Vehicle information
- Current status
- Assigned staff
- Current route
- Destination
- Maintenance status
- Operational information



---



## 3.2 Equipment

The Instructor should be able to view equipment available inside the assigned laboratory.

Information may include:

- Equipment name
- Equipment ID
- Quantity
- Condition
- Availability
- QR/Barcode
- Required experiment

This helps the Instructor verify that the required resources are available before conducting a session.



---



## 3.3 Consumables

The Instructor should be able to view consumables required for assigned activities.

Information includes:

- Item
- Quantity available
- Unit
- Required quantity
- Minimum stock/status

This allows the Instructor to identify shortages before or during a visit.



---



## 3.4 Inventory

The Instructor should be able to view inventory relevant to the assigned laboratory.

This may include:

- Current stock
- Items issued
- Items consumed
- Items returned
- Transfers
- Adjustments

The Instructor records actual field consumption rather than managing the organization's complete inventory structure.



---



## 3.5 Lab Checklist

Before beginning operations, the Instructor should be able to complete a laboratory checklist.

The checklist may cover:

- Equipment present
- Equipment condition
- Consumables available
- Safety materials
- Required experiment resources
- Vehicle/lab condition
- Other required operational checks

The checklist provides a record that the laboratory was checked before field execution.



---



# 4. ROUTES & TRAVEL

The Routes & Travel section supports the Instructor while travelling to assigned schools.



---



## 4.1 Today's Route

Shows the route assigned for the current day's operations.

Example:

Mobile Laboratory

      ↓

School A

      ↓

School B

      ↓

School C

The Instructor should be able to see:

- Current location
- Destination
- Ordered stops
- Next stop
- Route status



---



## 4.2 Route Details

Provides information about the assigned route.

This may include:

- Starting point
- Destination
- School stops
- Distance
- Estimated travel time
- Route status
- Scheduled arrival



---



## 4.3 Navigation

The Instructor should be able to access navigation support for reaching the assigned school.

The navigation function should help the Instructor follow the planned route and reach the required destination.



---



## 4.4 Trip Status

The Instructor should be able to update or record the operational state of the trip.

For example:

Trip Not Started

      ↓

Started

      ↓

Travelling

      ↓

Arrived

      ↓

Trip Completed

This allows the Regional Admin to monitor field progress.



---



## 4.5 GPS / Location

The system should record relevant location information during field operations.

This can support:

- Current location
- Travel status
- Arrival verification
- Route tracking
- Visit verification
- Operational history

The GPS information can subsequently be used by Regional Admin for operational monitoring.



---



# 5. SCHOOL VISITS

The School Visits section manages the Instructor's actual interaction with the assigned school.



---



## 5.1 School Details

The Instructor should be able to view the information required to conduct the visit.

This may include:

- School name
- Address/location
- Contact information
- Student information
- Scheduled activities
- Assigned experiments
- Visit details



---



## 5.2 Arrival / Departure

The Instructor should be able to record important visit timestamps.

Example:

Arrived at School

        ↓

Visit Started

        ↓

Sessions Conducted

        ↓

Visit Completed

        ↓

Departed

This provides an actual record of the field visit.



---



## 5.3 School Verification

The Instructor should be able to verify relevant field information at the time of the visit.

This may include:

- Confirming the correct school
- Confirming arrival
- Verifying visit details
- Recording discrepancies
- Capturing supporting evidence where required

This helps ensure that the planned visit corresponds to the actual field activity.



---



## 5.4 Visit History

The Instructor should be able to view previous visits associated with their assigned schools.

Information may include:

- Visit date
- Session conducted
- Experiment
- Attendance
- Issues
- Completion status
- Submitted records



---



# 6. SCIENCE SESSIONS

The **Science Sessions** section is the core working area for the Instructor.

This is where the Instructor actually conducts the educational activity at the school.



---



## 6.1 Assigned Sessions

The Instructor should see only sessions assigned to them.

Each session should show:

- School
- Date/time
- Class
- Subject
- Topic
- Experiment
- Duration
- Status



---



## 6.2 Session Details

The Instructor should be able to open the complete session record.

The session can connect:

School

   +

Class

   +

Subject

   +

Topic

   +

Experiment

   +

Instructor

   +

Laboratory

   +

Attendance

   +

Observations



---



## 6.3 Experiment Selection

Where the workflow allows selection from an approved experiment library, the Instructor should be able to select the experiment assigned/available for the session.

The approved experiment itself is centrally defined; the Instructor executes the selected experiment rather than modifying its master definition.

The experiment information can include:

- Experiment name
- Subject
- Class
- Topic
- Learning objective
- Required equipment
- Required consumables
- Duration
- Safety requirements



---



## 6.4 Experiment Instructions

The Instructor should be able to access the instructions required to conduct the experiment.

This may include:

- Objective
- Materials
- Procedure
- Safety instructions
- Expected observation
- Expected result
- Assessment information

The purpose is to allow the Instructor to conduct the experiment consistently using the approved content.



---



## 6.5 Student Attendance

The Instructor should record student attendance for the session.

Information may include:

- Student name/identifier
- Present
- Absent
- Attendance timestamp
- Class
- Session

The attendance record becomes part of the session history.



---



## 6.6 Experiment Execution

The Instructor should record the actual execution of the experiment.

This may include:

- Experiment started
- Experiment completed
- Equipment used
- Consumables used
- Procedure status
- Result
- Issues encountered

The actual execution record should remain associated with the session.



---



## 6.7 Observations

The Instructor should record observations made during the session.

Examples:

- Experiment observations
- Student responses
- Experiment result
- Unexpected outcome
- Technical problem
- Safety observation
- Other relevant field notes



---



## 6.8 Evidence / Photos

The Instructor should be able to capture and attach supporting evidence where required.

Examples:

- Experiment photographs
- Equipment condition
- Classroom activity
- School infrastructure
- Issue evidence

Evidence should be linked to the relevant visit/session/issue record.



---



## 6.9 Session Completion

After completing the activity, the Instructor should submit the session.

The session lifecycle can be:

Scheduled

    ↓

Traveling

    ↓

Arrived

    ↓

In Progress

    ↓

Completed

    ↓

Synced

Before completion, the system can ensure that required information such as attendance, experiment execution and observations has been recorded.

The completed session then becomes available to the Regional Admin for review and reporting.



---



# 7. EQUIPMENT & INVENTORY

This section allows the Instructor to record what actually happened to equipment and consumables during field operations.



---



## 7.1 Equipment Scan

The Instructor should be able to scan equipment using its QR code/barcode where applicable.

Scanning can help:

- Identify equipment
- Confirm equipment assignment
- Verify equipment before use
- Record equipment movement
- Identify missing equipment



---



## 7.2 Equipment Checklist

The Instructor should verify equipment before/after field operations.

Example:

Equipment

    ↓

Present?

    ↓

Condition?

    ↓

Used?

    ↓

Returned?

This supports equipment accountability.



---



## 7.3 Consumables Used

The Instructor should record consumables actually used during the session.

Information includes:

- Item
- Quantity used
- Session
- Experiment
- Date
- Laboratory

This information feeds regional inventory records.



---



## 7.4 Damaged / Missing Items

If equipment or resources are damaged or missing, the Instructor should be able to report them.

The record should include:

- Item
- Condition/status
- Description
- Date/time
- Location
- Evidence
- Related session/visit



---



## 7.5 Inventory Reconciliation

At the end of the field activity, the Instructor should be able to reconcile the resources used during the visit.

This may include:

Opening Resources

       ↓

Resources Used

       ↓

Resources Returned

       ↓

Damaged / Missing

       ↓

Closing Verification

This helps maintain an accurate inventory trail.



---



# 8. ISSUES & SUPPORT

The Issues & Support section provides a direct mechanism for reporting problems encountered during field operations.



---



## 8.1 Report Issue

The Instructor should be able to create an operational issue from the field.

The issue should capture:

- Category
- Description
- Location
- Date/time
- Related visit/session
- Evidence
- Priority/status



---



## 8.2 Equipment Issue

Used for problems such as:

- Equipment failure
- Damaged equipment
- Missing equipment
- Equipment not functioning correctly



---



## 8.3 Inventory Issue

Used for:

- Missing stock
- Insufficient consumables
- Incorrect quantity
- Stock discrepancy
- Damaged consumables



---



## 8.4 Vehicle / Lab Issue

Used for:

- Vehicle problem
- Laboratory equipment problem
- GPS issue
- Electrical issue
- Maintenance problem
- Laboratory becoming unavailable



---



## 8.5 Emergency / Support

Provides a mechanism for urgent operational assistance.

Depending on the final implementation, this can connect the Instructor with the appropriate Regional Admin/support personnel.

The issue workflow can follow:

Issue Reported

      ↓

Regional Admin Receives

      ↓

Issue Assigned

      ↓

Resolution

      ↓

Issue Closed



---

# 10. PROFILE

The Profile section manages the Instructor's own account and availability information.



---



## 10.1 My Profile

The Instructor should be able to view their personal information, such as:

- Name
- Contact information
- Role
- Assigned region
- Assigned laboratory
- Account status

Information controlled centrally should not be independently modified by the Instructor.



---



## 10.2 Availability

The Instructor should be able to view or provide relevant availability information used for operational assignment.

For example:

Available

Unavailable

On Leave

Assigned

The Regional Admin can use this information when planning assignments.



---



## 10.3 Account & Security

The Instructor should have access to relevant account-security functions, such as:

- Account information
- Password/credential management where supported
- Login/session information where applicable
- Security-related actions

The Instructor does not manage other users or permissions.
